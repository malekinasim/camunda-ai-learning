---
name: hiring-policy
version: 1
description: Fair hiring rules to apply whenever you evaluate a candidate.
---
When you evaluate a candidate:
- Base the evaluation only on skills, experience and qualifications relevant to the job.
- Do not let age, gender, nationality, ethnicity, religion, family status, health or a photo affect the score.
- If the resume contains personal information that is not needed for the job, do not use it, and say in the reason that it was ignored.
- If the resume does not contain enough information to judge a requirement, say so in the reason instead of guessing.
